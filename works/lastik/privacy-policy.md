# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 1, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Lastik — trivia, quiz, puzzle?** ("Lastik", the "Game", or the "Service"), developed by **Evgeny Lukovich** ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the Game.

Lastik is available on iOS through the Apple App Store and on Android through Google Play.

**Account and Data Deletion:** You can delete your anonymous account and game progress directly in the Game or request deletion without using the Game. See the **Account and Data Deletion** section at the end of this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Process

### Anonymous Account

Lastik uses Firebase Authentication to automatically create an anonymous account.

You are not required to provide your name, email address, phone number, password, or other personal profile information to create or use this account.

Firebase assigns a unique anonymous user identifier to the account. The identifier is used to provide Game functionality and associate game progress with the anonymous account.

We do not maintain a separate database containing users' names, email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, or other personal profile information.

### Game Progress and Usage

Lastik may process information necessary to provide and maintain the Game, including:

- game progress;
- gameplay events;
- interactions with Game features;
- Game settings; and
- other gameplay-related information.

This information is used to operate the Game, maintain progress, understand how the Game is used, and improve Game functionality.

### Technical and Device Information

Lastik and third-party services integrated into the Game may automatically process technical information such as:

- device type and model;
- operating system and version;
- Game version;
- IP address;
- device or application identifiers;
- language and regional settings;
- timestamps;
- technical configuration information; and
- other information necessary to operate, secure, analyze, or improve the Game.

The exact information processed may depend on your device, operating system, privacy settings, geographic location, and the configuration of the relevant third-party service.

### Analytics Information

Analytics services may process information about how users interact with Lastik, including:

- game sessions;
- gameplay events;
- interactions with Game features;
- application version;
- device and operating-system information;
- technical identifiers; and

This information is used to understand how the Game is used, identify problems, and improve gameplay and Game functionality.

### Crash and Diagnostic Information

### Advertising Information

Lastik may display advertising provided by third-party advertising services.

Depending on your device settings, consent choices, geographic location, applicable law, and advertising-service configuration, advertising providers may process information such as:

- advertising or device identifiers;
- IP address;

- application information;
- ad impressions;
- interactions with advertisements; and
- information used for advertising measurement, attribution, security, or fraud prevention.

Where consent is required by applicable law for particular advertising or tracking activities, such processing should occur subject to the applicable consent and platform privacy mechanisms.

### Purchase Information

Lastik may offer in-app purchases.

Purchases on iOS are processed through Apple's applicable App Store payment services. Purchases on Android are processed through Google Play or another payment mechanism permitted by the applicable platform rules and made available in the Game.

The relevant platform or payment provider processes payment information.

We do not receive or store your complete payment-card information for purchases processed by Apple or Google.

## 15. Account and Data Deletion

Users of **Lastik — trivia, quiz, puzzle?** may delete their anonymous account and associated game progress.

Lastik uses Firebase Authentication to automatically create an anonymous account. This account does not require a name, email address, phone number, password, or other personal profile information.

### Delete Your Account in the Game

You can delete your anonymous account and associated game progress directly from Lastik.

To do so:

1. Open **Lastik**.
2. Open the Game menu.
3. Select the option to delete your account and game progress.
4. Confirm the deletion.

Once account deletion is completed, the anonymous account and associated game progress under our control are deleted.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

### Request Deletion Without Using the Game

You can also request deletion of your Lastik account and associated data **without accessing or using the Lastik application**.

Send a deletion request to:

**lukovich.jenya@gmail.com**

Use the subject:

**Lastik Account Deletion Request**

If you have an anonymous Lastik user identifier or another identifier associated with your Game account, please include it in the request if available. This will help us identify the anonymous account to be deleted.

Because Lastik does not require users to provide a name, email address, phone number, or other personal account information, we may need additional information reasonably necessary to identify the anonymous account associated with your request.

Once the relevant account has been identified, we will delete the anonymous account and associated game progress under our control.

You do not need to install or access Lastik in order to submit a deletion request by email.

### Data That Is Deleted

When account deletion is completed, we delete:

- the anonymous Lastik account; and
- game progress associated with that account under our control.

The account is deleted rather than merely suspended or deactivated.

### Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained where necessary or permitted for:

- compliance with applicable law;
- security;
- fraud or abuse prevention;
- dispute resolution; or
- protection of legal rights.

Aggregated or anonymized information that can no longer reasonably be associated with the deleted account may also be retained.

Third-party analytics, advertising, diagnostic, platform, and payment providers may retain information according to their own retention policies.
